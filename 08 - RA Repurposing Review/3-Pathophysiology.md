### جملة 3

```
PPAR-γ ↓ in human RA FLS (TNF-α ↑)
TNF-α-induced FLS: pioglitazone, rosiglitazone or PPAR-γ overexpression
→ IL-1β, IL-6, IL-8 ↓; IL-10 ↑
PPAR-γ bound p53 (Co-IP); linked to p53 signaling (KEGG)
[LiXF2023]
```
