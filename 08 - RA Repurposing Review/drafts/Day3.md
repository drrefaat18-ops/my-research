## 4.1.3. GLP-1 Receptor Agonists
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.1c-GLP1-RA]] · 🎯 ~300 كلمة
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:

GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RAs), such as liraglutide, semaglutide and dulaglutide, raise insulin release when glucose is high, lower glucagon, slow stomach emptying and increase fullness [Israel2026]. Their link to RA comes from two sides. RA patients without diabetes have an impaired incretin-insulin axis, as found in 151 patients compared with 210 sex-matched controls [TejeraSegura2017]. GLP-1 receptors are also found on immune cells (T and B cells, monocytes and macrophages), where the drugs lower TNF-α, IL-6 and IL-1β, block NF-κB and favour M2 (anti-inflammatory) macrophages [Israel2026].

Clinical data in RA come mainly from a single-centre chart review of overweight or obese patients (BMI ≥ 27 kg/m²) that compared 173 patients who took semaglutide or tirzepatide (a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist) with 42 patients who were prescribed one of these drugs but never took it [Kellner2025]. Disease activity, graded from clinic notes on a 0–3 scale rather than DAS28, changed by −0.03 versus +0.20 in controls (_P_ = 0.03), which is probably too small to matter clinically [Kellner2025]. Pain fell by 0.6 cm on a visual analogue scale in the treated group but rose by 1.3 cm in controls (_P_ < 0.001) [Kellner2025]. ESR and CRP did not fall more than in controls, and the pain relief was not linked to weight loss [Kellner2025]. The study was small, and the reasons for starting the drug were not measured [Kellner2025].

Not all data point to a benefit. In a case-control study of 4,535 RA patients and 22,675 controls, use of semaglutide or liraglutide in the 10 years before diagnosis was linked to higher odds of new-onset RA (adjusted odds ratios 1.64 and 1.58), but dulaglutide was not [Israel2026]. This link was seen mainly with short use (≤6 months), and the authors suggest the drugs may cause a short-lived rise in inflammation early in treatment [Israel2026]. Confounding may explain part of this, because obesity and diabetes, the main reasons for giving these drugs, raise RA risk on their own [Israel2026]. The authors add that longer use may lower inflammation through weight loss and better insulin sensitivity [Israel2026]. This could explain why patients in the Kellner study, followed for up to a year, had less pain rather than worse disease.

So far, the evidence is small, observational and mixed, and we found no randomized trials in RA. GLP-1RAs may help some obese RA patients, but they cannot be recommended yet, and future trials should check whether the first months of treatment briefly worsen inflammation.

## 4.1.4. SGLT2 Inhibitors
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.1d-SGLT2i]] · 🎯 ~250 كلمة
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:

SGLT2 inhibitors are oral drugs for type 2 diabetes; dapagliflozin, for example, blocks sodium–glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) in the kidney, so less glucose goes back into the blood [ElDemerdash2025]. In rats with arthritis induced by complete Freund's adjuvant, El-Demerdash et al. tested three oral daily doses of dapagliflozin (1, 5 and 10 mg/kg) for 3 weeks; the highest dose worked best [ElDemerdash2025]. Compared with untreated arthritic rats, dapagliflozin alone lowered serum NF-κB p65 by 66% and TNF-α by 71% [ElDemerdash2025]. It raised the p-AMPK/t-AMPK ratio in the joint by 374%, while methotrexate alone did not change it [ElDemerdash2025]. This is the same AMPK pathway described for metformin (Section 4.1.1). The drug also switched on autophagy: ATG-7, Beclin-1 and ULK-1 rose in the joint [ElDemerdash2025]. On X-ray, neither drug alone reduced bone erosion or joint-space narrowing; only the two drugs together did [ElDemerdash2025].

In humans, Yen et al. used TriNetX records to emulate target trials in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, comparing SGLT2 inhibitors with sulfonylureas, DPP-4 inhibitors or pioglitazone (more than 80,000 matched pairs per comparison) [Yen2026]. In the first year, new RA was slightly less common with SGLT2 inhibitors than with sulfonylureas (hazard ratio 0.90; 95% CI 0.84–0.97) [Yen2026]. Against DPP-4 inhibitors (HR 0.91) or pioglitazone (HR 0.92), the difference was not significant [Yen2026]. The gap with sulfonylureas shrank with longer follow-up and lost significance [Yen2026]. The authors point out that one year is short, because RA has a long silent phase before diagnosis [Yen2026]. These data also concern RA prevention in people with diabetes, not treatment of established RA.

Unlike GLP-1RAs (Section 4.1.3), SGLT2 inhibitors were not linked to a higher RA risk. Still, we found only one animal study and one observational study, and no trial in patients who already have RA. SGLT2 inhibitors need more animal work and randomized trials in RA before any use in patients.
