## 4.1.3. GLP-1 Receptor Agonists
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.1c-GLP1-RA]] · 🎯 ~300 كلمة
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RAs), such as liraglutide, semaglutide, and dulaglutide, mimic the actions of endogenous GLP-1, leading to increased glucose-dependent insulin secretion, reduced glucagon secretion, delayed gastric emptying, and enhanced satiety [Israel2026]. Notably, dysregulation of the incretin-insulin axis has been observed in patients with rheumatoid arthritis even in the absence of diabetes. In a cross-sectional study involving 151 non-diabetic patients with RA and 210 sex-matched controls, impairments in the incretin-insulin axis were identified among patients with RA, suggesting that alterations in this metabolic pathway may be intrinsically linked to the disease process [Tejera-Segura2017]. Supporting a potential role for GLP-1RAs in RA, preclinical studies have demonstrated direct anti-inflammatory effects within the joint. In fibroblast-like synoviocytes derived from patients with RA and stimulated with IL-1β, lixisenatide reduced the production of key pro-inflammatory cytokines, including TNF-α, IL-6, and IL-8, while also suppressing the expression of matrix metalloproteinases, enzymes that contribute to cartilage degradation [Israel2026]. In addition to these local effects, GLP-1RAs may directly modulate immune responses through activation of GLP-1 receptors expressed on a variety of immune cells, including monocytes, macrophages, and T and B lymphocytes, providing a potential mechanism for broader immunoregulatory and anti-inflammatory actions [Israel2026]. Clinical evidence has also begun to emerge from real-world practice. In a single-centre retrospective chart review of overweight or obese patients with RA (BMI ≥ 27 kg/m²), 173 patients treated with semaglutide or tirzepatide were compared with 42 patients who had been prescribed, but did not receive, a GLP-1RA, providing an opportunity to evaluate the potential impact of these agents on disease outcomes in routine clinical care [Kellner2025].
