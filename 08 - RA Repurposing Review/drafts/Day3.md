## 4.1.3. GLP-1 Receptor Agonists
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.1c-GLP1-RA]] · 🎯 ≥300 كلمة (حد أدنى)
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:

Liraglutide, semaglutide and dulaglutide are receptor agonists of glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1RAs), which raise insulin release when glucose is high, lower glucagon, slow gastric emptying and increase satiety [Israel2026]. Two lines of evidence link these drugs to RA. In a cross-sectional study of 151 patients with RA but without diabetes and 210 sex-matched controls, the patients had an impaired incretin-insulin axis, with higher GLP-1, glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and insulin levels and more insulin resistance [TejeraSegura2017]. GLP-1 receptors are also found on immune cells (T and B cells, monocytes and macrophages) [Israel2026]. There, the drugs have been reported to lower TNF-α, IL-6 and IL-1β, block nuclear factor kappa B (NF-κB) and favor M2 (anti-inflammatory) macrophages [Israel2026].

Clinical data in RA come mainly from a single-center chart review of overweight or obese patients (BMI ≥ 27 kg/m²) [Kellner2025]. It compared 173 patients who took semaglutide (most patients) or tirzepatide (a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist) with 42 patients who were prescribed one of these drugs but did not take it [Kellner2025]. Disease activity, graded from clinic notes on a 0–3 scale rather than DAS28, changed by −0.03 versus +0.20 in controls (P = 0.03), a small difference of unclear clinical meaning [Kellner2025]. Pain fell by 0.6 cm on a visual analogue scale in the treated group but rose by 1.3 cm in controls (P < 0.001) [Kellner2025]. ESR and CRP did not fall more than in controls, and the pain relief was not linked to weight loss, but the study was small and the reasons for starting the drug were not measured [Kellner2025].

Not all data indicate a benefit. A case-control study compared 4,535 RA patients with 22,675 controls and found that use of semaglutide or liraglutide in the 10 years before diagnosis was linked to higher odds of new-onset RA (adjusted odds ratios 1.64 and 1.58) [Israel2026]. Dulaglutide showed a similar but non-significant trend (adjusted odds ratio 1.31) [Israel2026]. This link was seen mainly with short use (≤6 months), and the authors suggest the drugs may cause a short-lived rise in inflammation early in treatment [Israel2026]. However, confounding may explain part of this, because obesity and diabetes, the main reasons for giving these drugs, raise RA risk on their own, and the authors add that longer use may lower inflammation through weight loss and better insulin sensitivity [Israel2026]. The two studies also address different questions, new-onset RA versus established RA, and their results are therefore not necessarily in conflict.

To date, the evidence is limited, observational and mixed, and we found no randomized trials in RA. GLP-1RAs may help some patients with RA and obesity, but they cannot be recommended yet, and future trials should check whether the first months of treatment briefly worsen inflammation.

## 4.1.4. SGLT2 Inhibitors
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.1d-SGLT2i]] · 🎯 ≥250 كلمة (حد أدنى)
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:

SGLT2 inhibitors, such as dapagliflozin, are oral drugs for type 2 diabetes that block glucose reabsorption in the kidney [ElDemerdash2025]. The only animal study in arthritis that we found reported clear anti-inflammatory effects. In an Egyptian study (Ain Shams University) of AIA rats induced with complete Freund's adjuvant, three oral daily doses of dapagliflozin (1, 5 and 10 mg/kg) were tested for 3 weeks; the highest dose worked best [ElDemerdash2025]. Compared with untreated arthritic rats, dapagliflozin alone lowered serum NF-κB p65 by 66% and TNF-α by 71% [ElDemerdash2025]. It raised the p-AMPK/t-AMPK ratio in the joint by 374%, whereas MTX alone did not change it, which suggests involvement of the AMPK pathway described for metformin (Section 4.1.1) [ElDemerdash2025]. It also raised joint gene expression of the autophagy markers autophagy-related gene 7 (ATG-7), Beclin-1 and unc-51-like kinase 1 (ULK-1) [ElDemerdash2025]. On X-ray, neither drug alone reduced bone erosion or joint-space narrowing; only the two drugs together did [ElDemerdash2025].

Human data are limited to RA onset in people with diabetes: a study used TriNetX records to emulate target trials in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes [Yen2026]. It compared SGLT2 inhibitors with sulfonylureas, dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) inhibitors or pioglitazone (more than 80,000 matched pairs per comparison) [Yen2026]. In the first year, SGLT2 inhibitor use was linked to a slightly lower risk of new RA than sulfonylurea use (0.118% vs 0.131%; HR 0.90, 95% CI 0.84–0.97), whereas the differences against DPP-4 inhibitors (HR 0.91) and pioglitazone (HR 0.92) were not significant [Yen2026]. The gap with sulfonylureas shrank with longer follow-up and lost significance, as it also did in a per-protocol analysis [Yen2026]. The authors suggest that one year may be too short, because RA has a long silent phase before diagnosis [Yen2026].

Unlike GLP-1RAs (Section 4.1.3), SGLT2 inhibitors were not linked to a higher RA risk than other glucose-lowering drugs [Yen2026]. Still, we found only one animal study and one observational study and no trial in patients who already have RA [ElDemerdash2025][Yen2026]; their use in RA therefore remains experimental.

## 4.4. Emerging Candidates
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.4-Emerging]] · 🎯 ≥200 كلمة (حد أدنى)
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:

Several other drugs have been proposed for RA, but their evidence is weak, indirect or mixed (Table 2). In rats with CIA, the non-selective β-blocker propranolol reduced inflammation in the heart and made arrhythmias less likely [Lin2020]. However, its effect on the joints was small: paw swelling and clinical scores stayed close to those of untreated CIA rats and improved only mildly late in the disease [Lin2020]. In pooled data from five trials of tocilizumab and/or csDMARDs (5,502 RA patients), the 594 (10.8%) who used β-blockers, mostly β1-selective ones, reached remission on the Simplified Disease Activity Index (SDAI) less often (adjusted HR 0.70, 95% CI 0.57–0.87) [Abuhelwa2021].

Data for the psychiatric drugs are weaker still. Sertraline, a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI), had no effect on synovitis, cartilage damage or bone erosion in CIA rats, unlike MTX, infliximab and tocilizumab in the same study [Chmielewski2026]. Valproate has been proposed because it inhibits histone deacetylases (HDACs), and two selective inhibitors of one isoform (HDAC6) lowered arthritis scores in CIA mice [Oh2017]. However, these inhibitors differ from valproate, and we found no clinical study of valproate in RA.

For DPP-4 inhibitors, the data concern RA risk rather than treatment. A nested case-control study of diabetic patients starting second-line therapy (790 RA cases and 11,850 controls) found that, compared with other second-line antidiabetic drugs, DPP-4 inhibitor use was not linked to new RA after adjustment (adjusted OR 1.16, 95% CI 0.94–1.43) [Kathe2018]. In patients who did not use insulin, the odds were higher (OR 1.30, 95% CI 1.001–1.985) [Kathe2018]. We found no RA-specific study of allopurinol, and for now these drugs remain hypotheses or have mixed results; better animal studies should come before any trial in patients with RA.
