## 4.1.2. Pioglitazone
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.1b-PPAR-agonists]] · 🎯 ~450 كلمة
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:
> 

Pioglitazone, a thiazolidinedione, activates the nuclear receptor PPAR-γ (peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor-gamma). Although it is mainly used as an antidiabetic drug, it may also have anti-inflammatory effects. PPAR-γ activation suppresses pro-inflammatory genes and the production of inflammatory cytokines, matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs), and reactive oxygen species (ROS) [Shahin2011]. Another reason to test it in RA is that insulin resistance and inflammation make each other worse [Ormseth2013].

In animals, pioglitazone helped only at high doses or when combined with methotrexate. In the adjuvant-induced arthritis (AIA) rat model, oral pioglitazone reduced disease severity, but only at the top dose of 30 mg/kg/day [Koufany2008]. It reduced bone erosions and inflammation-associated bone loss but failed to prevent cartilage lesions [Koufany2008]. These anti-arthritic doses were also higher than those needed to restore insulin sensitivity, and they increased body fat [Koufany2008]. In a later study using the same model, pioglitazone alone (1.35 mg/kg/day) had no significant effect on disease activity [Roy2017]. Combined with methotrexate (MTX; 0.225 mg/kg/day), it lowered disease activity more than either drug alone (P < 0.05) [Roy2017].

Clinical trials show a small benefit at best. An Egyptian nonrandomized, single-blind trial enrolled 49 patients with RA and type 2 diabetes, all maintained on MTX 15 mg/week [Shahin2011]. The 28 patients with more active disease added oral pioglitazone 30 mg/day for 12 weeks, while the other 21 took placebo [Shahin2011]. At 12 weeks, DAS28 (P = 0.001) and CRP (P < 0.0001) were lower in the pioglitazone group than in the placebo group, but ESR stayed higher, and weight gain occurred in 25% of treated patients [Shahin2011]. The placebo group also improved from baseline, and the two groups did not start with the same disease activity, which weakens this result [Shahin2011].

In a US crossover trial that was randomized, double-blind and placebo-controlled, 34 patients with RA on stable therapy received pioglitazone 45 mg/day and placebo for 8 weeks each [Ormseth2013]. Pioglitazone produced a modest 9.3% fall in DAS28-CRP (P = 0.046) and no significant change in DAS28 using erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR; P = 0.92) [Ormseth2013]. Lower-extremity edema occurred in 16% of participants while on pioglitazone but in none while on placebo [Ormseth2013].

A larger crossover trial with the same design and dose enrolled 143 non-diabetic patients with RA, with 3 months per treatment arm [Marder2013]. DAS28-CRP decreased by a mean of 0.31 on pioglitazone compared with 0.15 on placebo (P = 0.02) [Marder2013]. However, the authors concluded that these findings did not, at that stage, support adding PPAR-γ agonists to the standard treatment of RA [Marder2013]. The benefit in these trials was small and came mainly from CRP-based scores, not from ESR or joint counts [Ormseth2013][Marder2013]. Given the risk of fluid retention and weight gain, pioglitazone should not be used routinely in RA. It may still be studied as an add-on for patients with RA who also have insulin resistance or type 2 diabetes.



## 4.3. Angiotensin II Receptor Blockers
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.3-ARBs-Losartan]] · 🎯 ~300 كلمة
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:
> 

The renin–angiotensin system (RAS) regulates blood pressure and fluid balance, and its overactivity is a common cause of hypertension. However, some studies suggest that RAS also contributes to RA, particularly through angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE), which is overexpressed in the peripheral blood and synovium of patients with RA [Wang2013].

Angiotensin II acts on angiotensin II type 1 receptors (AT1R). This produces ROS and switches on NF-κB, and NF-κB then raises the release of several pro-inflammatory cytokines [Mostafa2020]. In contrast, angiotensin II type 2 receptors (AT2R) work against AT1R, and their stimulation has been linked to anti-inflammatory and tissue-protective effects [Wang2013].

Animal and cell studies of losartan point in the same direction. In AIA rats, losartan at 10 and 15 mg/kg significantly lowered the polyarthritis index compared with vehicle [Wang2013]. This effect was smaller than that of MTX [Wang2013]. Losartan also shifted the balance between the two receptors, with less AT1R and more AT2R in the spleen and synovium, and this shift tracked the fall in the polyarthritis index [Wang2013]. Another study on AIA rats found that losartan lowered serum IL-6 and TNF-α compared with the untreated arthritic group, together with a rise in serum glutathione (GSH) and a fall in serum malondialdehyde (MDA) [AbdelElGaphar2018]. An in vitro study tested losartan, valsartan, and enalapril on peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) collected from 16 patients with RA [Cardoso2018]. Losartan lowered four cytokines (IL-6, IL-17F, IL-22, and IFN-γ), while valsartan and enalapril had few or no effects, so this effect may not be shared by valsartan [Cardoso2018]. However, only one high concentration (100 μM) was tested, and cells from patients with active disease responded less [Cardoso2018].

The main clinical evidence for this class in RA comes from a trial of another angiotensin II receptor blocker (ARB), candesartan. In this Egyptian single-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial, 45 patients with RA (15 per group) were treated for 3 months with add-on candesartan (8 mg/day), atorvastatin (20 mg/day), or placebo [Mostafa2020]. Compared with the control group, candesartan significantly lowered serum IL-1β, TNF-α, and CRP and improved DAS28 and HAQ-DI disability scores, while ESR fell only compared with baseline [Mostafa2020]. Unlike losartan in rats, candesartan did not significantly change MDA or rheumatoid factor (RF), while atorvastatin (Section 4.2.1) lowered both [Mostafa2020]. However, patients were grouped according to their other medical conditions, only 45 of 59 eligible patients were analysed, the groups differed at baseline in several variables, including CRP and HAQ-DI, and follow-up was short [Mostafa2020].

ARBs, particularly losartan, may be a sensible choice for patients with RA who also need treatment for hypertension, although only larger and longer trials of losartan can show this.
