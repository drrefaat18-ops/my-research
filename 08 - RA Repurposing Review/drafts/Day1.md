## 4.1.1. Metformin
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.1a-Metformin]] · 🎯 ~400 كلمة
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده: 
Metformin is an affordable first-line drug for type 2 diabetes with a good safety profile. Its main mechanism is AMPK activation, which regulates glucose and lipid metabolism. Metformin also has anti-inflammatory effects [Son2014]. AMPK activation suppresses mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR), and this modulates T-cell activity. For example, metformin inhibits the differentiation of Th17 cells, which are important drivers of chronic inflammation [Son2014].

In a preclinical collagen-induced arthritis (CIA) model, arthritic mice were given 5 mg/mouse of metformin orally 3 times per week for 9 weeks. This treatment significantly reduced arthritis severity and restored the Th17/Treg balance, decreasing Th17 activity and increasing Treg activity through reduced STAT3 and increased STAT5 phosphorylation. In the same model, the drug also suppressed osteoclastogenic activity [Son2014].

In a different model in which CIA rats were given intraperitoneal (i.p.) injections of metformin 100 mg/kg every 3 days, metformin protected the joints, as examined by micro-computed tomography (micro-CT) and histopathological analysis of rat knee joints. Micro-CT showed that metformin protected the trabecular bone, while histology showed reduced osteoclast formation and synovitis. Metformin also reduced systemic inflammation, chondrocyte apoptosis and cartilage matrix degradation [Fan2020].

In joint samples from RA patients, AMPK-α1 levels were higher than in samples from osteoarthritis patients, and its active (phosphorylated) form was linked to higher DAS28 and TNF-α. In cultured fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS) from these patients, metformin slowed cell growth and movement, and the cells released less IL-6 and IL-1β, as well as less TNF-α [Chen2020].

In a randomized, single-blinded clinical trial, a total of 66 Egyptian patients with moderately or highly active rheumatoid arthritis were enrolled. Of these, 33 patients received metformin 850 mg twice daily in addition to csDMARDs, and the other 33 patients received placebo. This led to reduced CRP levels and DAS28-CRP scores after 6 months compared to placebo, and only 3 patients withdrew from the metformin group because of GI side effects [Gharib2021]. In a prospective cohort study, 124 patients received metformin in addition to traditional csDMARD therapy for 90 days and were compared with 98 patients receiving csDMARDs only. This was associated with a faster fall in IL-6, TNF-α, HMGB1 and CRP than in the csDMARD-only group [Zhang2023].

In a large UK retrospective cohort of 224,551 patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus, metformin users showed no reduction in RA incidence [Zemedikun2021]. However, a Mendelian randomization study suggested that metformin may protect against rheumatoid arthritis [Liang2023]. Metformin may be a safe, affordable add-on to csDMARDs for reducing RA disease activity. However, its role in preventing RA remains uncertain, and larger, longer double-blind RCTs with radiographic outcomes are needed to confirm its benefit.



## 4.2.1. Statins
> 📖 الإملاء: [[4.2a-Statins]] · 🎯 ~450 كلمة
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:
HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors (statins) are widely used in cardiovascular therapy. Statins act mainly by competitively inhibiting the HMG-CoA reductase enzyme, thus reducing de novo cholesterol synthesis [Ren2023]. Statins also have effects other than lowering lipids; for example, by lowering isoprenoid synthesis, they reduce the isoprenylation of Ras and Rho, which are small GTP-binding proteins [Chodick2010][ElBarbary2010]. Statins suppress interferon-γ-induced MHC class II expression, and this reduces T-cell activation [Chodick2010]. Atorvastatin has been reported to reduce several proinflammatory mediators, such as IL-6 and TNF-α, the chemokine CCL2, and the NF-κB pathway [Sarabi2016]. Since T cells drive joint damage in RA, this T-cell modulation may help protect the joints [Xing2015]. IL-6 and TNF-α are key mediators of synovial inflammation, so their suppression may reduce joint inflammation [Sarabi2016]. Because RA patients have a higher cardiovascular risk, statins could also give them an extra cardiovascular benefit [Kitas2019].
In a preclinical study, mice with serum-induced arthritis were given atorvastatin and pravastatin at a dose of 0.2 mg/kg each (i.v.). Mice that were administered statins had reduced paw swelling and less knee destruction on histopathology [Walker2017].
Several earlier studies in CIA mice concluded that statins reduced arthritis [Vandebriel2012]. However, when Vandebriel et al. gave oral statins to CIA mice, they did not see improvement; instead, both atorvastatin and pravastatin accelerated disease onset [Vandebriel2012].
Regarding clinical trials, two meta-analyses showed that statins reduced disease activity, as measured by DAS28 (SMD −0.55 and −0.79) [Xing2015][Ren2023]. However, the included trials were small and mostly conducted at single centers [Xing2015], and both meta-analyses reported high heterogeneity [Xing2015][Ren2023]. In an unblinded randomized study conducted in Egypt, 30 early RA patients were divided into 2 groups. Group 1 received conventional RA therapy with methotrexate (MTX) and prednisone, while group 2 received MTX + prednisone + atorvastatin. DAS28 was significantly reduced from 6.09 to 3.9 in group 2 when compared to 6.19 to 5.27 in group 1 [ElBarbary2010]. In contrast, the largest trial, TRACE RA, conducted in the UK on 3,002 patients, found no difference in DAS28 between patients who received atorvastatin and those who received placebo [Kitas2019]. The study also deemed the lowering of CRP in the patients who received atorvastatin to be clinically insignificant in the context of RA [Kitas2019]. In the context of small trials, statins have been shown to have beneficial effects regarding the disease activity of RA; however, these effects have not been replicated in the largest trial. Thus, their clearest benefit in RA remains cheap and safe cardiovascular protection, and further investigations with disease activity as the primary end point are required to justify using statins in RA.
