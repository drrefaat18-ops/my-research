## 2. Methods
> 📖 الإملاء: [[2-Methods]] · 🎯 ≥200 كلمة (حد أدنى)
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:

For this narrative review, we searched one database, Europe PubMed Central (Europe PMC). Eight searches were run in the title and abstract fields, one per drug group: statins, angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs), fenofibrate, metformin, pioglitazone, GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RA), SGLT2 inhibitors and DPP-4 inhibitors. Each search, listed in full in Supplementary Table S1, paired the drug terms with "rheumatoid arthritis", "collagen-induced arthritis", "adjuvant-induced arthritis" or "inflammatory arthritis", and class terms such as thiazolidinedione and fibrate were added for most groups, whereas metformin was searched by name only. The searches were run on 6 October 2026 and repeated on 11 October 2026. Our description of the search covers item 3 of the Scale for the Assessment of Narrative Review Articles (SANRA), which is an appraisal scale for narrative reviews rather than a guideline for reporting them [Baethge2019].

Records indexed as reviews were filtered out, but no date or language limit was set. Where two studies answered the same question, the more recent one (2022 onward) was preferred. Every included article was in English, and full texts came from open access or the Egyptian Knowledge Bank (EKB). Only the abstract was used for five studies, and two trials were known only from their registry records. Narrative reviews found outside the search supplied background and possible mechanisms, whereas three meta-analyses, one on metformin and two on statins, supplied pooled results. Among eligible records, randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and large cohort studies came first, then mechanistic animal studies. Because reasons for exclusion were not recorded at screening, this step may not be fully reproducible.

The eight searches returned 628 records on both runs (statins 384, metformin 79, pioglitazone 65, ARBs 40, fenofibrate 22, DPP-4 inhibitors 17, GLP-1 RA 12 and SGLT2 inhibitors 9), and removing 24 duplicates left 604 records for screening. Of these, 48 were included and 556 were not. In addition, 31 references came from outside the search (primary studies missed by the search terms, narrative reviews, meta-analyses, drug labels, trial registry records and two methods papers), and 79 were cited overall (Figure 4). Because only one database was searched, eligible studies indexed elsewhere may have been missed.
