## 2. Methods
> 📖 الإملاء: [[2-Methods]] · 🎯 ≥200 كلمة (حد أدنى)
> ✏️ اكتب تحت السطر ده:

This narrative review used a structured, documented literature search. A single database, Europe PubMed Central (Europe PMC), was searched in the title and abstract fields. The eight searches covered statins, angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs), fenofibrate, metformin, pioglitazone and three further glucose-lowering classes: GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RA), SGLT2 inhibitors and DPP-4 inhibitors. Most groups added class terms, such as thiazolidinedione for pioglitazone and fibrate for fenofibrate (the metformin search used the drug name alone), combined with the terms "rheumatoid arthritis", "collagen-induced arthritis", "adjuvant-induced arthritis" or "inflammatory arthritis". Full search strings are given in Supplementary Table S1, and the search was run on 6 October 2026 and re-run on 11 October 2026 with identical counts. The description of the search addresses item 3 of the Scale for the Assessment of Narrative Review Articles (SANRA), a six-item scale for appraising narrative reviews that is not intended as a reporting guideline [Baethge2019].

Records indexed as reviews were excluded at the search stage. No date limit or language filter was applied; recent studies (2022 onward) were preferred where alternatives existed, and all included articles were in English. Full texts were obtained through open access or the Egyptian Knowledge Bank (EKB); the abstract alone was used for five studies, and trial registry records were used for two trials. Narrative reviews identified outside the search were cited for background and mechanisms, and three meta-analyses (one on metformin and two on statins) were cited for pooled results. When choosing among eligible records, priority was given to randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and large cohort studies, followed by mechanistic animal studies; exclusion reasons were not recorded at screening.

The eight searches identified 628 records before duplicate removal. By group, the counts were statins 384, metformin 79, pioglitazone 65, ARBs 40, fenofibrate 22, DPP-4 inhibitors 17, GLP-1 RA 12, and SGLT2 inhibitors 9. After 24 duplicates were removed, 604 records were screened, 556 were not included, and 48 were included from the database search. A further 31 references came from other sources (primary studies outside the search, narrative reviews, meta-analyses, drug labels, trial registry records and two methods papers), and 79 references were cited in total, as shown in the flow diagram (Figure 4).
