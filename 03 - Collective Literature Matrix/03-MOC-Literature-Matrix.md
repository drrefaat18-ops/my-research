   *Scope:* Interactive tabular and card views filtering studies by design category, source sheet, and quality control status.
3. **`03 - Collective Literature Matrix/Research-Papers.base` (Obsidian Base View)**  
   *Scope:* Structured catalog of all 29 scientific papers extracted directly from primary PDF literature.
4. **[[Precedent-Curcumin-Prednisolone-Nanoparticles|Precedent 01: Curcumin + Prednisolone in Albumin NPs]]**  
   *Scope:* Demonstration of 50% steroid dose reduction, passive synovial ELVIS targeting, and prevention of steroid-induced hyperglycemia and osteopenia.

   *Scope:* Demonstration of 80% steroid reduction, SIRT1/PPAR activation, deacetylation of p65 Lys310, and preservation of osteocalcin.
6. **Primary Literature Extraction Notes (Studies 01 to 12):**
