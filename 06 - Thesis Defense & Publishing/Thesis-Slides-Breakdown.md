## Slide 9: Glucocorticoids are potent anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive agents that play several critical, but typically temporary, roles in RA management:"Bridge" Therapy: This is their primary role. When a patient is started on a slow-acting DMARD (like methotrexate), which can take weeks or months to become fully effective. Glucocorticoids provide rapid relief from pain, swelling, and stiffness. They "bridge" the patient until the DMARD starts working.

- The role of Glucocorticoids in RA


## Slide 10: Glucocorticoids are highly effective for managing rheumatoid arthritis (RA), capable of providing long-term low-dose maintenance for severe cases and even slowing joint damage at higher doses, like DMARDs.However, due to a wide range of serious, dose-dependent side effects (like metabolic and cardiovascular complications), their use is strictly limited to the lowest possible dose for the shortest possible duration.

- The role of Glucocorticoids in RA


## Slide 11: N-Palmitoylethanolamine (PEA) is an endogenous lipid mediator, chemically composed of palmitic acid and ethanolamine, which is naturally produced within the human body and found in dietary sources such as egg yolks, soybeans, and peanuts.

When used in combination, PEA can lower the required dose of stronger drugs like NSAIDs and opioids, thereby reducing their side effects.

## Slide 14: While PEA exerts its anti-inflammatory effects through multiple modes of action, its primary mechanism involves the activation of PPAR-α. This activation regulates key signaling pathways, leading to the inhibition of both NF-κB and p38-MAPK, which collectively reduce pro-inflammatory factors and modulate inflammation.

- PEA as an Anti-inflammatory
- Wang Y, Duan X, Li Z, Pan Y, Deng J. Palmitoylethanolamide in the Treatment of Pain and Its Clinical Application Prospects. Drug Des Devel Ther. 2025;19:6897-6923.


## Slide 15: The specificity of the NF-κB inhibition occurs through a novel transcriptional mechanism independent of classical PPAR Response Elements (PPREs). Instead, in a ligand-dependent manner, PPAR-α potentiates the ability of the NF-κB subunit p65 to activate the promoter for IκBα, the natural inhibitor of NF-κB. Thus, PPAR-α reduces inflammation by indirectly boosting IκBα expression, thereby suppressing NF-κB activity.

- PEA as an Anti-inflammatory

## Slide 22: Why This Is a Big Deal?Understanding this partnership opens exciting new possibilities for medicine, especially for treating chronic inflammatory diseases like asthma, rheumatoid arthritis, or inflammatory bowel disease.•The Problem with "Crisis Manager" (GR) Alone: Using steroid drugs (which activate GR) is very effective but has severe side effects like weight gain, diabetes, and bone thinning when used long-term.•The Potential Solution: Combining a lower, safer dose of a steroid (GR activator) with a PPARα-activating drug could be much more effective than either drug alone.
