## 1. Serum ACPA / anti-CCP

**Verdict: KEEP WITH CAVEAT.** **Evidence grade: Moderate** (multiple rat CFA-AIA primary studies, but all use human-kit-derived "anti-CCP" ELISAs of unvalidated rat specificity).

### 1a. Is ACPA detectable/elevated in rat CFA-AIA? — YES, repeatedly reported

- **Alghulami OM, Jasim GA, Jasim SY.** *Evaluating the docetaxel effect in an animal model of polyarthritis.* Inflammopharmacology. 2024 Jun;32(3):1827-1838. doi:10.1007/s10787-024-01459-2. **PMID 38619760.**
  > "Forty male Wistar rats... The Second Group to Fifth group were induced with Complete Freund's adjuvant... Level of ACPA, PAD4, TNF-α, IL-1β, and VEGF significantly decreased in the DTX group than induction group (p < 0.05)."
  This is the single most directly relevant paper to your protocol: CFA Wistar rats, ACPA **and** NLR **and** PLR measured together.

- **Shaaban HH, Hozayen WG, Khaliefa AK, El-Kenawy AE, Ali TM, Ahmed OM.** *Diosmin and Trolox Have Anti-Arthritic, Anti-Inflammatory and Antioxidant Potencies in Complete Freund's Adjuvant-Induced Arthritic Male Wistar Rats: Roles of NF-κB, iNOS, Nrf2 and MMPs.* Antioxidants (Basel). 2022 Aug 30;11(9):1721. doi:10.3390/antiox11091721. **PMID 36139795.** (Beni-Suef University, **Egypt**)
  > "...detection of rheumatoid factor (RF), anti-citrullinated protein antibodies (ACPA), tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α), interleukin-13 (IL-13), and interleukin-17 (IL-17) levels by enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA)... The two compounds significantly lowered the elevated serum levels of RF, ACPA, TNF-α, and IL-17."

- **Wang M, Zhao H, Zhao H, Huo C, Yuan Y, Zhu Y.** *Moxibustion-mediated alleviation of synovitis in rats with rheumatoid arthritis through the regulation of NLRP3 inflammasome by modulating neutrophil extracellular traps.* Heliyon. 2023 Dec 13;10(1):e23633. doi:10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e23633. **PMID 38187290.**
  > "...the levels of anti-cyclic citrullinated peptide antibody (CCP-Ab) and interleukin (IL)-1β were examined in rat serum with ELISA... AA modeling markedly increased JSD, AI, synovial protein expression of MPO, NE, Cit-H3, PAD-4, and NLRP3, and serum levels of CCP-Ab and IL-1β in rats (P < 0.01)."
  Mechanistically coherent: PAD4 and citrullinated histone H3 are demonstrably upregulated in AA rat synovium, i.e. there *is* a citrullination substrate in this model.

- **Yu X, Fu X, Du F, et al.** *Therapeutic Effect and Underlying Mechanism of Blue Mussel Oil on Adjuvant-Induced Rheumatoid Arthritis in Rats.* Nutrients. 2026 Jan 9;18(2):215. doi:10.3390/nu18020215. **PMID 41599830.**
  > "...reduced serum levels of rheumatoid factor (RF), anti-cyclic citrullinated peptide (anti-CCP) antibody, and pro-inflammatory cytokines such as TNF-α and IL-17."

### 1b. Is the validity of "rat anti-CCP" kits questioned? — YES, legitimately

- **Qin G, Lin X, Liang P, Li Y, Zhou C, Kutty SN, Rikard H.** *[Strong inflammation is essential for expression of articular cartilage-specific citrullinated antigens].* Nan Fang Yi Ke Da Xue Xue Bao. 2020 Aug 30;40(8):1081-1089. doi:10.12122/j.issn.1673-4254.2020.08.03. **PMID 32895186.**
  > "Luminex analysis showed that the anti-citrullinated protein antibody was lowly expressed in mouse serum... the expression of citrullinated epitopes is not sufficient to induce an immune response to anti-citrullinated antibodies."
  Rodent models generate **low to negligible genuine ACPA**; the cross-reactive signal commercial kits read in rodent serum is of uncertain specificity. (Mouse CIA + rat intra-articular LPS; not rat AIA.)

**Practical conclusion:** the measurement is *publishable* (precedent exists, including from an Egyptian group), but the discussion must say "anti-CCP-reactive IgG measured with a rat-adapted kit; the identity of the citrullinated epitope in the rat is not established." Do not claim ACPA as a diagnostic-equivalent biomarker.

### 1c. Is "AIA is purely T-cell/macrophage with no autoantibody role" accurate? — **NO. This claim is wrong as stated.**

The model is *T-cell-transferable*, but that does not exclude a humoral arm.

- **Pearson CM, Wood FD.** *Passive transfer of adjuvant arthritis by lymph node or spleen cells.* J Exp Med. 1964 Oct 1;120(4):547-560. doi:10.1084/jem.120.4.547. **PMID 14212118.**
  > "...passively transferred... by transfer of viable lymph node or spleen cells, but not thymus cells... Control studies using non-viable cells... were always negative. It is concluded that adjuvant arthritis is the result of an immunologic reaction which is perhaps similar to delayed hypersensitivity."
  This is the origin of the "cell-mediated" claim — note it says *perhaps similar to delayed hypersensitivity*, and that the antigen "remains obscure".

- **Feige U, Schulmeister A, Mollenhauer J, Brune K, Bang H.** *A constitutive 65 kDa chondrocyte protein as a target antigen in adjuvant arthritis in Lewis rats.* Autoimmunity. 1994;17(3):233-239. doi:10.3109/08916939409010659. **PMID 7948607.**
  > "**T cells and antibodies obtained from rats with adjuvant arthritis respond to chondrocyte membrane antigen(s). In Western blots a 65 kDa chondrocyte membrane protein (CH65) is stained by sera from arthritic rats.**"
  Direct primary evidence of an **autoantibody response in rat AIA**. This single citation refutes the "no autoantibody role" claim.

- Supporting (not rat AIA): **Šteigerová M, Šíma M, Slanař O.** *Pathogenesis of Collagen-Induced Arthritis...* Folia Biol (Praha). 2023;69(2):41-49. doi:10.14712/fb2023069020041. **PMID 38063000** — reviews RF, ACPA, anti-CII antibodies and B-cell subsets as part of rodent arthritis pathogenesis.

- Anti-HSP65 antibodies: hsp65 reactivity in AIA is documented primarily as a **T-cell** epitope (HSP65 180-188). A dedicated "anti-HSP65 antibody in rat AIA serum" primary study was **not located** (Q3, 11 hits, none rat-AIA-serology). **Mark: No direct evidence located — do not assert.**

**Egyptian-outsourcing caveat:** ACPA requires a single serum aliquot shipped to Cairo/Tanta; stable frozen at -20 °C for the short term. The real risk is **kit provenance**: insist the lab states manufacturer, catalogue number, declared species reactivity and standard curve range in the report, and keep that page for the thesis appendix. A reviewer will ask.

## 2. Serum TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6 at chronic time points

**Verdict: KEEP WITH CAVEAT** (serum is acceptable; joint homogenate is the stronger claim). **Evidence grade: Strong** for detectability in rat AIA serum.

- **Wu SD, Wu XJ, Wang TT, Jiang F, Hu MW, Li R, Liu J, Cai L.** *Protective effects of piperlongumine against adjuvant-induced arthritis in rats through modulating OPG/RANKL/NF-κB signaling pathway.* J Pharm Pharmacol. 2024 Jun 6;76(6):646-655. doi:10.1093/jpp/rgae015. **PMID 38334976.**
  > "...reductions in hind paw swelling, arthritis index, thymus/spleen index, ankle joint pathological damage, **production of TNF-α, IL-1β, and IL-6 in both serum and synovium**, and osteoclast formation."
  The gold-standard design: *both* compartments measured, and both move.

- **Tan LH, Shi YN, Sun SY, Qiao LN, Yan QQ, Yang YS.** *[Effect of electroacupuncture on articular morphological changes and serum ...]* Zhen Ci Yan Jiu. 2021 Aug 25;46(8):649-655. doi:10.13702/j.1000-0607.201205. **PMID 34472749.**
  > "The levels of serum TNF-α, IL-1β, and IL-6 were measured by ELISA... Following modeling, the paw volume, AI score, number of osteoclasts, contents of serum TNF-α, IL-1β and IL-6... [increased]." (28-day intervention.)

- **Li J, Wang L, Zeng G, Li H, Luo J, Tian Q, Zhang ...** Immunopharmacol Immunotoxicol. 2022 Dec;44(6):959-969. doi:10.1080/08923973.2022.2093738 *(DOI as printed truncated in record; PMID authoritative)*. **PMID 35737007.**
  > "...and IL-6 in the rats' serum were determined by ELISA. Western blotting was used to detect the protein expression of TLR4 and NF-κB in the rat ankle tissue."

- **El-Shiekh RA, El-Mekkawy S, Mouneir SM, Hassan A, Abdel-Sattar E.** *Therapeutic potential of russelioside B as anti-arthritic agent in Freund's adjuvant-induced arthritis in rats.* J Ethnopharmacol. 2021 Apr 24;270:113779. doi:10.1016/j.jep.2021.113779. **PMID 33418032.** (Cairo University, **Egypt**)
  > "The treatments were initiated from **day 16** when AIA model was established and continued up to **day 40**."
  > Cytokines reported as **"pg/mg protein"** — i.e. this Egyptian group measured NF-κB, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β **in tissue homogenate, not serum**, at exactly your treatment window (D16→D40).

**Pitfall / caveat.** A dedicated primary study reporting "serum cytokines below the limit of detection in late AIA" was **not located** (Q5 produced no such paper; a targeted query returned 0 hits). So the "serum cytokines are undetectable late" worry is **not supported by a citable primary source** — but neither is it refuted: every paper above reports *group-mean* differences, not individual-animal detectability, and sacrifice at day 46 is **later than any of these** (day 22-40). Treat this as a real but uncited risk.

**Recommendation:** El-Shiekh 2021 is both Egyptian and time-matched to your design — follow it and measure cytokines in **paw/joint homogenate (pg/mg protein)** as primary, with serum as secondary if budget allows. Homogenate also sidesteps the late-timepoint dilution problem entirely.

**Egyptian-outsourcing caveat:** cytokine ELISAs are the single most shipping-sensitive assay here. No -80 °C chain = freeze-thaw and proteolysis risk. Aliquot single-use, ship on dry ice in one batch, and have the Cairo/Tanta lab run all samples on **one plate per analyte** (inter-plate CV otherwise swamps the treatment effect in a 5-6 group design). Joint homogenate must be made with protease inhibitor and shipped frozen — this is harder than serum, and is the main argument *for* keeping serum as the pragmatic primary.

## 3. Knee joint histopathology — knee vs ankle

**Verdict: WEAK as written — switch primary histology site to the ANKLE/hind paw.** **Evidence grade: Strong** that the ankle is the standard and maximally affected site in rat CFA-AIA.

### Where is inflammation maximal in CFA-AIA?

- **Pearson CM, Wood FD.** *Studies of arthritis and other lesions induced in rats by the injection of mycobacterial adjuvant. VII. Pathologic details of the arthritis and spondylitis.* Am J Pathol. 1963 Jan;42(1):73-95. **PMID 13942247** (PMC1949690). *(Scanned-image record; full text not machine-readable via Europe PMC — quote UNAVAILABLE, cited as the canonical pathologic description of AIA joint distribution.)*

- **Conway JG, Wakefield JA, Brown RH, et al.** *Inhibition of cartilage and bone destruction in adjuvant arthritis in the rat by a matrix metalloproteinase inhibitor.* J Exp Med. 1995 Aug 1;182(2):449-457. doi:10.1084/jem.182.2.449. **PMID 7629505.**
  > "In this model, pronounced acute and chronic synovial inflammation, **distal tibia and metatarsal marrow hyperplasia** associated with osteoclasia, severe bone and cartilage destruction, and ectopic new bone growth are well developed by 3 wk after adjuvant injection... **Radiological and histological ankle joint evaluation** on day 22 revealed a profound dose related inhibition of bone and cartilage destruction."
  The lesion is localised to the **tibiotarsal/metatarsal** complex — the ankle and hind paw.

- Convergent practice across the field (Q6, Q8, Q9, Q22): in CFA/AIA rat studies the histology site is **near-universally the ankle joint**. Examples retrieved 2026-10-02: Shaaban 2022 (PMID 36139795) "right hind **ankle** regions... dissected out and fixed in 10% neutral-buffered formalin for histological examination"; Gowayed 2020 (PMID 32791152) "Histopathological examination of the **ankle joint**"; Wu 2024 (PMID 38334976) "**ankle joint** pathological examination"; Wang 2024 (PMID 38187290) "synovium of rat **ankle** joints".

- **Bendele A, McComb J, Gould T, McAbee T, Sennello G, Chlipala E, Guy M.** *Animal models of arthritis: relevance to human disease.* Toxicol Pathol. 1999 Jan-Feb;27(1):134-142. doi:10.1177/019262339902700125. **PMID 10367688.** — reference text for model selection and histologic endpoints.

### Knee as a site — precedent exists but is secondary

Knee synovium **is** used in rodent arthritis histology, but typically alongside the ankle, and mostly in CIA or in the *antigen*-induced (intra-articular) knee model:
- Yuan J, Hu L, Song XG, Tang ZL, Zhang CY, Yu S, You YM. *[Influence of Moxibustion on TLR4-MyD88-NF-κB Signal Transduction Pathway of Synovial Tissue in Rheumatoid Arthritis Rats].* Zhen Ci Yan Jiu. 2015 Jun;40(3):199-204. **PMID 26237970** — CFA foot injection, but synovium read from the **knee**:
  > "the synovial tissue of the **knee-joint** was impaired remarkably and infiltrated by numerous inflammatory cells, and the synovial surface got thickening because of hyperplasia in the model group."
- J Ethnopharmacol. 2023 Dec 5;317:116850. **PMID 37385573** — CIA rats, "histopathology of **ankle joint and synovium from knee joint**" (both).

### Scoring systems and Safranin-O

- J Ethnopharmacol. 2025 Sep 25;353(Pt A):120321. **PMID 40714059.**
  > "Clinical parameters including **paw volume, arthritis score**, and inflammatory pain were monitored. **Histological analyses of ankle joints were performed using safranin-O staining and Mankin scoring.**"
- Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2022 May 14;604:22-29. **PMID 35279442.**
  > "On day 28, the **ankle joints** of the rats were removed and stained with **haematoxylin, eosin, and Safranin O**. Arthritic changes... cartilage destruction, and bone erosion were evaluated by **histological scoring**."

**Safranin-O role:** proteoglycan (glycosaminoglycan) stain — loss of red/orange cartilage staining is the standard readout of cartilage matrix depletion; feeds Mankin or a 0-4 cartilage-damage subscore. It is a cheap, routine histology stain with no instrument requirement beyond a light microscope — fully compatible with Port Said.

**Recommendation:** make the **ankle/tibiotarsal joint the primary histology site** (H&E + Safranin-O, semiquantitative scoring of synovial hyperplasia, inflammatory infiltrate, pannus, cartilage damage, bone erosion). Keep the knee only as an optional secondary site if blocks are already cut. Defending "knee as primary" in a CFA-AIA viva is an avoidable fight.

**Egyptian-outsourcing caveat:** decalcification (formic acid or EDTA) and paraffin processing of hind limbs is routine and locally available; FFPE blocks are the cheapest, most durable archive you have and are the one sample type with no cold-chain risk. Cut extra unstained slides at the time of sectioning — recutting later costs a second shipment.

## 4. NF-κB p65 nuclear IHC on decalcified joint

**Verdict: KEEP.** **Evidence grade: Moderate-to-Strong** (multiple rat arthritis studies; nuclear-specific scoring less consistently described).

- **Wu SD, et al.** J Pharm Pharmacol. 2024;76(6):646-655. doi:10.1093/jpp/rgae015. **PMID 38334976.** — rat AIA, ankle/synovium:
  > "...ankle joint pathological examination, **immunohistochemistry assay**, polymerase chain reaction, and western blot assay... PLM prevented IκBα degradation and phosphorylation, resulting in a reduced expression of the **nuclear NF-κB p65 protein** in AIA rat synovial tissues."
  Directly supports **nuclear** p65 as the scored compartment in AIA.

- **Yuan J, et al.** Zhen Ci Yan Jiu. 2015;40(3):199-204. **PMID 26237970.** — CFA rat, knee synovium:
  > "...the **NF-κB p 65 immunoactivity was assayed by immunohistochemistry**."

- **Sheng Z, Zeng J, Huang W, Li L, Li B, Lv C, Yan F.** *Comparison of therapeutic efficacy and mechanism of paclitaxel alone or in combination with methotrexate in a collagen-induced arthritis rat model.* Z Rheumatol. 2022 Mar;81(2):164-173. doi:10.1007/s00393-020-00940-x. **PMID 33320289.** (CIA, not AIA)
  > "...expression was detected by **immunohistochemistry**... **TLR4 and p-NF-κBp65** protein expression in synovial tissue..."
  Note this group scores **phospho-p65**, which is a cleaner activation readout than total p65.

- Shaaban HH, et al. Antioxidants. 2022;11(9):1721. **PMID 36139795** — CFA Wistar, Egypt: NF-κB p50 and **p65 by Western blot** on ankle tissue (an alternative if IHC antibody sourcing fails).

**Scoring methods:** the retrieved AIA/CIA literature reports p65 IHC as semiquantitative immunoreactivity/expression scores rather than a standardised nuclear-translocation index. A defensible, zero-cost method: count **% p65-positive *nuclei*** among synovial lining and sublining cells in 5 high-power fields per section, two blinded readers, ImageJ for counting. State the scoring rule explicitly in Methods — this is where reviewers push back, not on the antibody.

**Feasibility / Egypt caveat:** p65 IHC on **EDTA-decalcified** FFPE is the safer route. Strong acid decalcification (formic/nitric) degrades nuclear epitopes and is the commonest cause of a failed p65 IHC on bone. Specify EDTA decalcification in the protocol *before* the blocks are made — it cannot be fixed retrospectively. Antigen retrieval and antibody are outsourced; send 2-3 extra unstained slides per animal so a failed run can be repeated without reshipping blocks.

## 6. p38 MAPK ELISA on joint homogenate

**Verdict: WEAK as proposed (total p38). Reformulate or drop.** **Evidence grade: Strong — against total p38 as an endpoint.**

Every retrieved rat arthritis study reads **phosphorylated** p38, never total p38 alone:

- J Ethnopharmacol. 2020 Oct 5;260:113039. doi:10.1016/j.jep.2020.113039. **PMID 32497675** (rat AIA, joint synovia):
  > "...the **autophosphorylation of ERK1/2, JNK and p38 in joint synovia of rats**... It also suppressed the **phosphorylation** and gene level of TNF-α-induced signaling molecules, as ERK1/2, JNK, and p38 in synovium."
- J Ethnopharmacol. 2013 Jul 30;148(3):842-850. doi:10.1016/j.jep.2013.05.023. **PMID 23711830** (CIA rat):
  > "...(ERK), Jun N-terminal kinase (JNK) and **p38 MAPKs were examined by immunohistochemistry staining and western blot analysis**... SCE inhibited **p-ERK, p-JNK and p-p38** expression."
- Arthritis Rheum. 2013 Nov;65(11):2814-2825. doi:10.1002/art.38095. **PMID 23897050**:
  > "In vivo findings showed increased expression of ADAM-17 and **phospho-p38 MAPK**."
- Food Funct. 2017 Dec 13;8(12):4668-4674. doi:10.1039/c7fo01311f. **PMID 29160881**:
  > "The protein expression levels of **phosphorylated p38, p-p65**..."
- Int Immunopharmacol. 2013 Dec;17(4):1037-1043. **PMID 24455774**:
  > "It also inhibited the **phosphorylation of p38** mitogen-activated protein [kinase]."

**Does total p38 change with treatment?** No retrieved study reports total p38 as a treatment-responsive endpoint; in all of them total p38 is the loading denominator for p-p38/total-p38. **Measuring total p38 by ELISA will almost certainly produce a flat, uninterpretable result and will be read by examiners as a methodological error.**

**Phospho-epitope stability in shipped homogenates:** this is the fatal practical problem. Phosphorylation states require homogenisation in **phosphatase inhibitor** buffer and snap freezing, then an unbroken frozen chain. With no liquid nitrogen and no reliable -80 °C, a p-p38 result from a homogenate trucked Port Said → Cairo/Tanta is not defensible, and a reviewer who knows the assay will say so. *(This is a well-established property of phospho-epitopes; no AIA-specific primary citation located — stated as a methodological constraint, not an evidenced claim.)*

**Recommendation: DROP the p38 ELISA.** It costs money, it measures the wrong analyte (total), and the right analyte (phospho) is not survivable in your logistics. If a MAPK readout is required, use **p-p38 IHC on the FFPE ankle block** instead — fixation locks the phospho-epitope at the moment of sacrifice, which is exactly what shipping a homogenate does not.

## 7. Oxidative stress panel (MDA/TBARS, GSH, SOD, catalase, NO/nitrite, TAC)

**Verdict: KEEP — this is the strongest, cheapest, most defensible block in the whole protocol.** **Evidence grade: Strong** (multiple concordant rat CFA/AIA primary studies).

- **Shaaban HH, Hozayen WG, Khaliefa AK, El-Kenawy AE, Ali TM, Ahmed OM.** Antioxidants (Basel). 2022;11(9):1721. doi:10.3390/antiox11091721. **PMID 36139795.** (CFA male Wistar, **Egypt**)
  > "...as well as **markers of oxidative stress and the antioxidant defense system in serum**... The compounds stimulated higher activities of antioxidants, such as **glutathione, glutathione-S-transferase, catalase, and superoxide dismutase, and reduced lipid peroxidation in the serum** of arthritic rats."
  Exact template: CFA Wistar, **serum** panel, Egyptian lab.

- **Anwar I, Sharif A, Ahmad M, Shabbir M, Akhtar B.** *Farnesol alleviates inflammatory responses and oxidative stress in Freund's complete adjuvant induced arthritic rat model...* Inflammopharmacology. 2025 Jun;33(6):3085-3098. doi:10.1007/s10787-025-01787-x. **PMID 40439876.**
  > "Farnesol significantly increased the enzymatic activity of **catalase (CAT) and superoxide dismutase (SOD)**, as well as the non-enzymatic activity of **glutathione (GSH)**. In addition, farnesol reduced **tissue lipid peroxidation and nitric oxide (NO)** synthetase activity, which was evident by the reduced levels of oxidative stress markers **malondialdehyde (MDA)**... and **nitrite**."
  Note: **tissue** lipid peroxidation + **paw thickness** + body weight — a design very close to yours.

- **Kumar VL, Roy S.** *Calotropis procera latex extract affords protection against inflammation and oxidative stress in Freund's complete adjuvant-induced monoarthritis in rats.* Mediators Inflamm. 2007;2007:47523. doi:10.1155/2007/47523. **PMID 17497032.**
  > "...a marked reduction in the levels of **glutathione, catalase, superoxide dismutase and glutathione peroxidase** and an increase in the lipid peroxidation as indicated by the higher levels of **thiobarbituric acid reactive substances (TBARSs)**." — measured **in the joint**.

- **El-Shiekh RA, et al.** J Ethnopharmacol. 2021;270:113779. **PMID 33418032.** (Cairo University)
  > "The **total antioxidant capacity (TAC)** was significantly restored to normal values in RB, 50 mg/kg treated rats (4.01 ± 0.09 nmol/mL vs. 3.71 ± 0.27 nmol/mL) and the levels of myeloperoxidase (MPO) reduced by 10-folds."
  TAC precedent, Egyptian, D16-D40 window.

- **Wang Y, Zhao X, Wang J, Zhu X.** *Norisoboldine Reduces Arthritis Severity by Attenuating Inflammation, Oxidative Stress, and Extracellular Matrix Degradation in a Rat Model of Rheumatoid Arthritis.* J Inflamm Res. 2024 Nov 15;17:8839-8852. doi:10.2147/JIR.S476824. **PMID 39564548.**
  > "NOR also exhibited strong antioxidant effects, demonstrated by **decreased MDA levels and enhanced SOD, catalase, and GPx activities**."

**Sample choice:** both compartments have precedent — **serum** (Shaaban 2022, El-Shiekh TAC) and **paw/joint tissue homogenate** (Kumar 2007, Anwar 2025). Serum is cheaper, simpler and sufficient; joint homogenate is the stronger mechanistic claim. Pick serum as primary under the zero-spend filter.

**Instrumentation:** MDA/TBARS, GSH, SOD, catalase, nitrite (Griess) and TAC are all **colorimetric end-point assays read at a single visible wavelength (532, 412, 560, 240/510, 540, 505 nm)** on a standard **UV-Vis spectrophotometer** — **no ELISA plate reader required**. Egyptian Biodiagnostic (Giza) supplies exactly this panel as cuvette kits. **This is the only mechanistic block in the protocol that is fully executable in Port Said with equipment you already have.** Weight the thesis narrative accordingly.
*(Caveat: the specific-kit/manufacturer claim is operational knowledge, not a cited literature finding — verify the current Biodiagnostic catalogue yourself; no cost figures given.)*

## 8. Hematology indices NLR / SII

**Verdict: KEEP as an exploratory/secondary endpoint only — do NOT power the study on it.** **Evidence grade: Weak in rat AIA.**

### The one direct rat-CFA precedent is a *negative* result

- **Alghulami OM, Jasim GA, Jasim SY.** Inflammopharmacology. 2024;32(3):1827-1838. doi:10.1007/s10787-024-01459-2. **PMID 38619760.**
  > "**No significant difference in body weight, platelet-lymphocyte ratio, and white blood cell count between the groups. Neutrophile lymphocyte ratio showed weak correlation with ACPA**, while PAD4 showed good correlation with RA markers."
  CFA Wistar rats, NLR and PLR measured — and NLR performed **poorly**. This is both your precedent *and* your warning.

### NLR/SII are computable in rats, in other models

- Hamid SJ, Salih TM, Aziz TA. *Integrated computational design, synthesis, and evaluation of dipeptide-coumarin conjugates as anti-inflammatory agents.* RSC Adv. 2026 Aug 6;16(40):45989-46014. doi:10.1039/d6ra04487e. **PMID 42564775.** (rat **formaldehyde paw edema**, not AIA)
  > "...treatment with the higher dose resulted in a marked decrease in white blood cell count and key inflammatory indices, including the **neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR) and systemic immune-inflammation index (SII)**."
- Mu Y, Yang Y, Jiang S, Liu C, Han Y, Jiang J, Wang Y. *Benefits of Puerarin on Metabolic Syndrome...* Nutrients. 2024 Apr 25;16(9):1273. doi:10.3390/nu16091273. **PMID 38732519.** (rat metabolic syndrome, not arthritis)
  > "...PUE lowered systemic inflammatory indices including C-reactive protein (CRP), **neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR), monocyte-to-lymphocyte ratio (MLR), and systemic immune-inflammation index (SII)**."

**No rat AIA paper using SII was located.** NLR in rat AIA: one paper (Alghulami 2024), negative.

**Practical note:** requires **EDTA whole blood with an automated differential** (SII needs platelets + neutrophils + lymphocytes). Rat CBC also needs a veterinary analyser with rodent settings; a human-calibrated analyser will misclassify rat leukocytes.

**Recommendation:** collect it (an EDTA tube costs nothing and the blood is drawn anyway at sacrifice), report it as exploratory, and pre-register in the protocol that it is **not** a primary endpoint. Frame the Alghulami negative result in the discussion rather than letting a reviewer find it.

## 9. PEA mechanism — PPAR-α, and the Bougarne justification

**Verdict on "PPAR-α mediates PEA's anti-inflammatory effect in vivo": KEEP — Evidence grade: Strong (mouse).**
**Verdict on "Bougarne 2009 justifies not measuring PPAR-α/GR": WEAK — the citation does not do the work being asked of it.**

### 9a. PPAR-α as PEA's anti-inflammatory mechanism — established

- **Lo Verme J, Fu J, Astarita G, La Rana G, Russo R, Calignano A, Piomelli D.** *The nuclear receptor peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor-α mediates the anti-inflammatory actions of palmitoylethanolamide.* Mol Pharmacol. 2005 Jan;67(1):15-19. doi:10.1124/mol.104.006353. **PMID 15465922.**
  > "PEA selectively activates PPAR-α in vitro with an EC50 value of 3.1 ± 0.4 μM... In two animal models, **carrageenan-induced paw edema and phorbol ester-induced ear edema, PEA attenuates inflammation in wild-type mice but has no effect in mice deficient in PPAR-α**."
  The KO-mouse loss-of-function design is what makes this Strong. **Caveat: mouse, acute edema models — not rat, not chronic arthritis.**

- **LoVerme J, La Rana G, Russo R, Calignano A, Piomelli D.** *The search for the palmitoylethanolamide receptor.* Life Sci. 2005 Aug 19;77(14):1685-1698. doi:10.1016/j.lfs.2005.05.012. **PMID 15963531.** (review)

- **D'Agostino G, La Rana G, Russo R, et al.** *Acute intracerebroventricular administration of palmitoylethanolamide, an endogenous PPAR-α agonist, modulates carrageenan-induced paw edema in mice.* J Pharmacol Exp Ther. 2007 Sep;322(3):1137-1143. doi:10.1124/jpet.107.123265. **PMID 17565008.**
  > "**The obligatory role of PPAR-α in mediating the effects of PEA was confirmed by the lack of the compounds anti-inflammatory effects in mutant mice lacking PPAR-α.**"
  > ⚠️ **SERIOUS FLAG:** the PubMed record carries **two Expressions of Concern (J Pharmacol Exp Ther. 2026 Apr;393(4):103834 and 393(4):103823).** **Do not cite this paper in the thesis.** Use Lo Verme 2005 (Mol Pharmacol) instead, which carries no such notice.

- Joint-relevant PEA evidence (closer to your model):
  - Inflammopharmacology. 2021 Oct;29(5):1475-1486. **PMID 34468900** — *"Anti-inflammatory activity of palmitoylethanolamide ameliorates osteoarthritis"*: "**PEA reduced knee joint swelling and cartilage degradation**"; "Oral administration of PEA had no adverse effects on the BW, liver, or kidneys." (MIA rat knee OA — rat, oral, joint; different disease.)
  - Pharmacol Res. 2019 Jul;145:104264. doi:10.1016/j.phrs.2019.104264. **PMID 31063807** — "**Increased NAAA expressions and decreased PEA levels in synovial membrane**... by directly increasing PEA levels in joints."

### 9b. What Bougarne 2009 actually shows

- **Bougarne N, Paumelle R, Caron S, Hennuyer N, Mansouri R, Gervois P, Staels B, Haegeman G, De Bosscher K.** *PPARα blocks glucocorticoid receptor α-mediated transactivation but cooperates with the activated glucocorticoid receptor α for transrepression on NF-κB.* Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 2009 May 5;106(18):7397-7402. doi:10.1073/pnas.0806742106. **PMID 19376972.**
  > "**Simultaneous activation of PPARα and GRα dose-dependently enhances transrepression of NF-κB-driven gene expression and additively represses cytokine production.** In sharp contrast and quite unexpectedly, **PPARα agonists inhibit the expression of classical glucocorticoid response element (GRE)-driven genes** in a PPARα-dependent manner, as demonstrated by experiments using PPARα wild-type and knockout mice... treatment with the PPARα agonist **fenofibrate prevents glucocorticoid-induced hyperinsulinemia of mice fed a high-fat diet**."

**What it is:** principally **cell-based transfection/reporter and gene-expression work** (NF-κB and GRE reporter assays, cytokine production, RNA Pol II / GRα promoter recruitment), with **PPARα WT vs KO mice** and a **mouse metabolic in vivo experiment** (fenofibrate + glucocorticoid, high-fat diet). It uses **fenofibrate/synthetic PPARα agonists, not PEA.** No arthritis. No rat. No joint.

**Does it justify not measuring PPAR-α/GR directly?** **No — and it arguably argues the opposite.** Bougarne is the reference you would cite to *explain* a PEA + prednisolone interaction, and what it predicts is **two-directional**: additive anti-inflammatory transrepression **and** antagonism of GRE-driven transactivation. If your study's whole point is PEA **plus low-dose prednisolone**, Bougarne makes the PPARα-GR interface a *hypothesis you are testing*, not a settled fact you can assume away.

**Honest, defensible position for the thesis** (and the lowest-cost one):
> "PPAR-α is the established mediator of PEA's anti-inflammatory actions (Lo Verme 2005, PPAR-α-null mice). The present study was designed to test the *functional* consequence of combining PEA with low-dose glucocorticoid at the level of downstream NF-κB-driven inflammation; direct quantification of PPAR-α and GR expression was beyond the resources of this facility and is acknowledged as a limitation."

That is a **limitation**, not a justification — and stated as a limitation it is far safer in a viva than a Bougarne citation a reviewer will check. Under RULE 0 (zero spend), the limitation framing costs nothing and survives peer review; the overclaim does not.

- **Anwar I, et al.** Inflammopharmacology. 2025;33(6):3085-3098. **PMID 40439876.**
  > "Hematological tests, **paw thickness, body weight**, and antioxidative status were measured."

### Body weight

Universally reported as both an efficacy and a toxicity/welfare endpoint — see PMID 28892928, 35279442, 40439876, and note **Alghulami 2024 (PMID 38619760): "No significant difference in body weight... between the groups"**, i.e. do not expect body weight to discriminate treatments in a short CFA study.

**Recommendation:** **plethysmometer is the citable standard**; **digital caliper paw thickness is an acceptable, cheaper substitute with its own primary precedent (Anwar 2025)**. If no plethysmometer is available in Port Said, use a digital caliper, measure dorsoventral thickness at a marked anatomical landmark, take the mean of 3 readings per paw by a **blinded** observer, and cite Anwar 2025 in Methods. The blinding and triplicate reading are what make the caliper defensible — not the instrument.
