CFA induction in the inguinal region, and the full **9-group experimental design** with 30-day oral dosing (Days 16–45).
2. **[[Main-Protocol-7-Groups|Experimental Design: Regimens, Ascending PEA Doses & Timeline]]**  
   *Scope:* Operational laboratory breakdown detailing the 7 active treatment arms (Prednisolone alone 10 mg/kg, m-PEA alone 200 mg/kg, Combo Tier 1 50+5 mg/kg, Combo Tier 2 100+5 mg/kg, Combo Tier 3 200+5 mg/kg, Tacrolimus 1.8 mg/kg, and Baricitinib 10 mg/kg) alongside parallel vehicle controls.

   *Scope:* Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for preparing uniform micronized suspensions from commercial Egyptian *Limitless PEA* tablets using analytical mortar pulverization.
4. **[[Pilot-Study-Rationale-and-Protocol|Pilot Study Dosing Strategy & Objectives (from pilot.docx)]]**  
   *Scope:* Internal exploratory protocol ($n=2$ harvested pairs) testing feasibility, preliminary safety, and baseline induction kinetics. Quarantined with `[!WARNING]` stamps.

   *Scope:* Quantitative organ safety panel (AST, ALT, urea, creatinine, glucose) and preliminary inflammatory suppression ($76.9\%$ IL-6 suppression, $61.2\%$ CRP reduction) in high-dose PEA + Prednisolone combination. Strictly quarantined ($n=2$).
