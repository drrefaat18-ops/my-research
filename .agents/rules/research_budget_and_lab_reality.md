Whenever formulating advice, proposing assays, or assessing methodology for Dr. Refaat Ibrahim's Master's thesis:

## 1. Budget: 100% Self-Funded Out-of-Pocket
- There is **no research grant, departmental budget, or university subsidy**.
- All expenses (rats, Limitless PEA, Prednisolone, Baricitinib, Tacrolimus, CFA, outsourced lab fees, histology slides, ELISA kits) are paid directly from the researcher's personal salary/savings.
- **Rule (Zero Spend Filter):** Every proposed assay must pass a strict cost-to-impact evaluation. Never suggest luxury or exploratory panels (e.g. DUSP1 RT-qPCR, Phospho-p38 ELISA, RNA-Seq) that could bankrupt the researcher. If an assay is not 100% vital for defense or publication, it is eliminated.

## 2. Infrastructure: Under-Equipped Local Facilities
- Port Said University animal house and faculty labs lack modern molecular biology infrastructure.
- There is no liquid nitrogen at the moment of animal sacrifice, no -80°C continuous cold-chain transport, and no in-house automated ELISA readers.

## 3. Operations: Mandatory Assay Outsourcing
- All specialized assays (ELISA, IHC, histology, clinical chemistry) must be outsourced to external commercial or academic referral laboratories in Cairo or Tanta.
- **Rule:** Prioritize specimen matrices that survive transport:
  1. **Formalin-Fixed Paraffin-Embedded (FFPE) blocks:** Durable at ambient temperature for years; ideal for H&E, Safranin-O, and NF-κB p65 / Caspase-3 IHC.
  2. **Frozen Serum Aliquots:** Durable when transported in standard ice/dry-ice boxes for ELISA and spectrophotometry.
  3. **Strictly Avoid:** Fragile fresh-frozen decalcified joint tissues for RNA or phosphoproteins due to inevitable degradation during inter-city transit.

## 4. Prioritize Affordable Local & Zero-Cost Solutions
- **Zero-Cost Novelty:** Maximize secondary parameters from collected routine data: individual-animal NLR (Neutrophil-to-Lymphocyte Ratio) and SII calculated from WBC differentials at zero additional financial cost.
- **Affordable Egyptian Kits:** Leverage local Egyptian reagent suppliers (e.g. Biodiagnostic spectrophotometric kits for metabolic markers: Fasting Glucose, Lipid Profile, AST, ALT at ~100–250 EGP per bottle) to prove glucocorticoid-sparing safety at rock-bottom cost.
