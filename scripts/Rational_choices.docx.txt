Rational to design the study
Induction agent:
Adjuvant-Induced Arthritis (AA) Model is the most suitable option for the following reasons:
Cheap
Rapid, highly reproducible study focused squarely on demonstrating the anti-inflammatory synergy between PEA and prednisolone, the AA model is an excellent choice due to its potent, innate-driven inflammation and direct precedent in steroid-sparing research. While the CIA model investigates whether the PEA/prednisolone combination can modify the long-term autoimmune course of the disease and provide protection against chronic joint destruction
Adjuvant-Induced Arthritis represents a classical model particularly well-established in rat studies The induction protocol involves:
CFA Preparation: Complete Freund's Adjuvant containing 1mg/mL heat-killed mycobacteria
Administration: Single intradermal injection 0.3ml into the footpad.
Disease Onset: Arthritis typically develops within 10-14 days post-injection The AA model demonstrates robust systemic inflammation with both articular and extra articular manifestations, including significant paw swelling, joint destruction, and occasional systemic features like weight loss and splenomegaly.
The following study acts as precedent for dose reduction of prednisolone using a nutraceutical and it utilizes AA as the RA model. “Morvaridi A, Abtahi Froushani SM, Farshid AA. Benefits of combining piperine with prednisolone in an experimental model of rheumatoid arthritis.”
Study Groups, Doses, Animals:
Based on the following study “Morvaridi A, Abtahi Froushani SM, Farshid AA. Benefits of combining piperine with prednisolone in an experimental model of rheumatoid arthritis.” The following has been established:
Male Wistar rats, aged 8 weeks, these rats had an average weight of 150 ± 7.00 g.
Oral route of administration of prednisolone and I.P 10% ethanol.
5 groups were used, however for the purpose of our study I suggest adding 2 groups to demonstrate a dose dependent effect of PEA + Prednisolone synergism. Groups are as follows:
Group 1 (Control): Healthy rats, no treatment.
Group 2 (RA): RA-induced rats, no treatment.
Group 3 (RA + PEA): RA-induced rats treated with PEA (50mg/Kg).
Group 4 (RA + Pred): RA-induced rats treated with Prednisolone (10.00 mg kg⁻¹).
Group 5 (RA+Com): RA-induced rats treated with a combination of: PEA (10 mg kg⁻¹) and Prednisolone (5.00 mg kg⁻¹).
Group 6 (RA+Com): RA-induced rats treated with a combination of: PEA (30 mg kg⁻¹) and Prednisolone (5.00 mg kg⁻¹).
Group 7 (RA+Com): RA-induced rats treated with a combination of: PEA (50 mg kg⁻¹) and Prednisolone (5.00 mg kg⁻¹).
The doses of PEA were determined using the following study “Jung JI, Lee HS, Jeon YE, Kim SM, Hong SH, Moon JM, et al. Anti-inflammatory activity of palmitoylethanolamide ameliorates osteoarthritis induced by monosodium iodoacetate in Sprague–Dawley rats. Inflammopharmacology. 2021;29:1475-86.”
Another paper used 30mg/kg orally of um-PEA Reduced serum levels of TNF-α, IL-1β, MCP-1, and LPS. Attenuated microglial and astrocyte activation, reduced pro-inflammatory pathways/cytokines in the brain, and decreased transcription of mast cell markers in the hippocampus	Lama, Adriano, et al. "Palmitoylethanolamide Dampens Neuroinflammation and Anxiety-like Behavior in Obese Mice." Brain, Behavior, and Immunity, vol. 102, 2022, pp. 110-23. PubMed, doi:10.1016/j.bbi.2022.02.008.
Study duration
Based on the following study “Morvaridi A, Abtahi Froushani SM, Farshid AA. Benefits of combining piperine with prednisolone in an experimental model of rheumatoid arthritis.” The following periods are established:
RA is to be induced on Day 0 with a single FCA injection.
Treatment is to start after manifestation of clinical symptoms determined by arthritic index (score of more than 1)
An average 28-30 days of treatment.
Parameters
Macroscopical parameters
Paw Volume: Measured every other day using a plethysmograph.
Arthritis Index: Scored every other day by observers based on swelling and redness.
Histopathology: The right ankle is to be sectioned and stained with Hematoxylin and Eosin (H&E) for examination under a light microscope to assess cartilage erosion, synovial hyperplasia, and inflammatory cell infiltration.
Cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6) ELISA on plasma samples.
