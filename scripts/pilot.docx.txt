Pilot Study: Establishing a Dosing Strategy for PEA and Prednisolone in a Rat Model of Rheumatoid Arthritis
1. Primary Objective:
To identify the most effective and synergistic dose combination of ultramicronized PEA and Prednisolone for suppressing inflammation and mitigating metabolic side effects, informing the design of a larger, definitive study.
2. Secondary Objectives:
To confirm the induction of arthritis and the baseline efficacy of a standard Prednisolone dose.
To gather initial data on the molecular mechanism (IκBα/NF-κB pathway).
To assess the feasibility of all experimental procedures.
Experimental Design
Animal Model:
Species/Strain: Female Wistar or Lewis rats (commonly used, robust responders).
Model: Collagen-Induced Arthritis (CIA). Induce arthritis with intradermal injection of Bovine Type II Collagen in Complete Freund's Adjuvant at the base of the tail.
Sample Size: n=4 per group. This small number is sufficient for a pilot to detect large effect sizes and trends without excessive resource use.
Groups and Dosing (All administered via I.P. injection):
The study will test two key doses of each drug in a factorial design.
Timeline:
Day 0: CIA Induction.
Day 14: Booster immunization (if required by your specific CIA protocol).
Day ~21: Start treatment when the first signs of arthritis (swelling, redness) appear in the majority of animals. Treat for 14 days.
Day ~35: End of study, tissue collection.
Outcome Measures to Be Analyzed with Kits
This pilot is designed to provide maximum data with minimal animals. You will process all samples from these animals as if it were the main study.
A) Clinical & Inflammatory Efficacy:
Arthritis Score: Standardized 0-4 scoring per paw for redness/swelling, several times per week.
Paw Volume: Measured with a plethysmometer before induction and at sacrifice.
Serum Cytokines (ELISA Kits): Analyze serum for IL-6, TNF-α, and MCP-1 at sacrifice. These are the key NF-κB targets mentioned in your PDF.
B) Molecular Mechanism (PPARα-NF-κB Cross-Talk):
Joint Tissue Homogenates: Collect ankle joints after sacrifice for protein extraction.
Western Blot or ELISA Kits: Measure protein levels of:
Phospho-IκBα (indicative of NF-κB activation)
Total IκBα (to see if PEA boosts its levels as per the mechanism)
PPARα (to confirm target engagement)
