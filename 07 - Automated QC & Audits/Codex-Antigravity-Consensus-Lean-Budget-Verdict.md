## 3. The Non-Negotiable Core Analytical Package

The core package that guarantees both thesis defense approval and Q1/Q2 consideration:

### A. Zero-Cost Longitudinal Clinical Phenotyping (0 EGP extra)
- **Paw swelling / joint volume:** Caliper or plethysmometer measurements every 3–4 days across the 28-day model. Calculate Area Under the Curve (AUC).
- **Standardized Arthritic Index (0–4 per paw, 0–16 total score):** Documented erythema and ankylosis progression.
- **Body weight gain/loss curve:** Primary surrogate for systemic disease burden and steroid cachexia.

### B. Gold-Standard Joint Histopathology & Cartilage Preservation (~27,000 – 31,000 EGP)
*Knee or tibiotarsal ankle joint (verify protocol-specified joint) processed into FFPE blocks (room-temperature stable).*
1. **H&E Staining (90 rats):** Blinded histological scoring across validated parameters: synovial hyperplasia, inflammatory cell infiltration, pannus formation, and bone erosion.
2. **Cartilage Stain (Safranin-O / Fast Green or Toluidine Blue):** Cut from the **exact same paraffin blocks** to quantify proteoglycan loss and chondrocyte depletion.

### C. Zero-Cost Hematology & Systemic Indices (0 EGP extra)
- From mandatory routine EDTA CBC:
  $$\text{NLR} = \frac{\text{Absolute Neutrophil Count}}{\text{Absolute Lymphocyte Count}}$$
  $$\text{SII} = \frac{\text{Platelet Count} \times \text{Neutrophils}}{\text{Lymphocytes}}$$
- Provides translational hematologic biomarkers without purchasing any commercial reagent.

### D. Targeted Local Metabolic Safety Screen (~4,000 EGP)
- Fasting blood glucose + ALT (using local Egyptian clinical chemistry kits/laboratories) to provide concrete evidence of the metabolic benefits of steroid reduction.
